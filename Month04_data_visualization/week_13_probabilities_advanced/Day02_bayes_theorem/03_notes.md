# Week 13 Day 2 — Bayes' Theorem

Bayes helps update probability after new evidence.

Prior:
Probability before seeing evidence.

Likelihood:
P(Evidence | Hypothesis)

Evidence:
Overall probability of the observed evidence.

Posterior:
Updated probability after seeing evidence.

Bayes:
P(H | E) = P(E | H) × P(H) / P(E)

Prior → Evidence → Posterior

Python:
*  → multiplication
/  → division

Bayes is useful for:
- classification
- fraud detection
- medical testing
- spam detection
- customer analytics
- risk analysis
