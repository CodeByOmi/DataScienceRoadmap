MATPLOTLIB

import matplotlib.pyplot as plt

Line:
plt.plot(x,y)

Bar:
plt.bar(x,y)

Histogram:
plt.hist(data,bins=10)

Scatter:
plt.scatter(x,y)

Title:
plt.title()

Labels:
plt.xlabel()
plt.ylabel()

Grid:
plt.grid()

Legend:
plt.legend()

Display:
plt.show()

Line → trends over time
Bar → compare categories
Histogram → distribution
Scatter → relationship between 2 variables
