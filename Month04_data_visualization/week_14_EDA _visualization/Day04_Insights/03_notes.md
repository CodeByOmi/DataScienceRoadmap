WEEK 14 — DAY 4

Pattern:
A repeated/noticeable behavior in data.

Insight:
A meaningful interpretation of a pattern for the business.

Univariate:
Analysis of one variable.

Bivariate:
Analysis of two variables.

Group comparison:
Use groupby() + aggregation to compare categories.

Outlier:
A value unusually far from most observations.

Segment analysis:
Analyze different groups separately.

Insight framework:
Observation
→ Possible explanation
→ Business implication
→ Next question

Important:
Correlation/association ≠ causation.

Good analyst:
Doesn't just describe charts.
Finds patterns, questions possible explanations,
and identifies what should be investigated next.
