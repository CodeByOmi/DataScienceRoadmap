WEEK 12 DAY 2 — VARIABILITY

Range:
Maximum - Minimum

Variance:
Measures spread using squared deviations from the mean.

Population variance:
np.var(data)

Sample variance:
np.var(data, ddof=1)

Standard deviation:
Square root of variance.

Population standard deviation:
np.std(data)

Low SD:
Values are closer to the mean.

High SD:
Values are more spread out.

Range uses only minimum and maximum.
Variance and SD use all observations.

Outliers can strongly increase variance and standard deviation.
